# GuestTrack for Claude

[GuestTrack](https://guesttrack.io) is visitor check-in for offices, schools, building sites, warehouses, events and
clubs: visitors scan a QR code at the door and fill in your Check-in form on their own phone, and you always know who
is on site.

This plugin connects Claude to your GuestTrack account and teaches it how GuestTrack is used, so you can:

- **Set up in one conversation.** Tell Claude what kind of site you run and where people arrive. It creates the
  project, its check-in points and its Check-in form, adds your hosts and groups, and tells you where to print the
  QR codes.
- **Run the front desk.** Check visitors in and out, see who is on site right now, log deliveries.
- **Invite visitors.** Send invites to one person or a list of up to 100, for a single visit or for regulars.
- **Stay safe.** See who is on site in an emergency and run a roll call.
- **Report.** Count visits by day, week, month or check-in point, and find any visit.

Claude asks before every change, and can't delete anything or change your billing, team or how long records are
kept. When you connect, you choose whether Claude may set up and change your projects.

## Getting started

1. Install the plugin and sign in with your GuestTrack account (or create a free one at
   [guesttrack.io](https://guesttrack.io)). In Claude Code:

   ```
   /plugin install guesttrack --marketplace guesttrack/claude-plugin
   ```

   In Claude (web, desktop and mobile), add it from the directory, or add the connector
   `https://api.guesttrack.io/mcp` under Settings › Connectors.
2. Ask, for example: "Set up GuestTrack for our primary school's front office", "Who is on site right now?",
   "Invite these five contractors for Monday 8am".

Every plan includes AI assistants, the free trial too.
